Plan Your Week

Students who plan their week feel less stressed. Write down your classes, assignments and exam dates at the start of every week.

Make a simple timetable

Give each subject a fixed time slot. Keep one slot free for catching up.

Set small goals

Finish one chapter or ten practice questions each day instead of studying everything at once.

Build Good Habits

Good habits matter more than studying for very long hours.

  • Study at the same place every day
  • Keep your phone away while studying
  • Take a 5 minute break after every 25 minutes
  • Sleep for at least 7 hours

Revise Regularly

Revision helps you remember what you learned. Here is a simple revision plan.

Simple revision schedule
When What to do
Same day Read your notes again for 10 minutes
After 1 week Solve practice questions
Before exams Do a full revision and a mock test

Quick Reminder

Understanding a topic is better than memorising it.
